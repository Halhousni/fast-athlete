The numbers behind it
You need

An EMG amplifier that exports CSV, TXT or MAT. You also need its sampling rate.

You get

A verdict for every muscle in the file: ready, go easy or rest.

It takes

A 30-second hold, then the upload. The app answers a few seconds later.

How it decides

As a muscle tires, its signal shifts to lower frequencies and grows in size. FAST compares the start of the hold with the end. The more both move, the lower the score.

This is a research prototype. The thresholds are provisional, and it does not replace a clinician.
